---

## Governance Risk

Whoever defines the invariants shapes what counts as drift.

Power asymmetries are part of invariant governance. They are not external noise.

In healthcare, some claimants have more operational power than others. Payers, regulators, institutions, and clinical systems may be able to make their invariant claims executable before patients can.

That does not make those claims more legitimate.

It makes the invariant process more vulnerable to capture.

---

## Healthcare Example

Health may be framed as:

- capacity to live on one's own terms
- functional restoration
- reduced suffering
- actuarial risk reduction
- population safety
- dignity, agency, and informed consent

No single claimant owns the invariant set.

---

## CANON Relevance

Invariant Governance defines the preserved goal-content.

CANON supplies the constraint-accountability machinery.

Healthcare invariant governance would ask:

- Which preserved goal does this constraint serve?
- Which claimant is authorized to define that goal?
- Is the invariant active, contested, retired, or failed?
- Has a metric replaced the thing it was meant to preserve?
- Has an institutional incentive shaped what counts as drift?
